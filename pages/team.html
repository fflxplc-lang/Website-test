Puttaswamy Gowda, CFP®

Founder & Chief Visionary Officer

Puttaswamy (Swamy) Gowda is the Founder and Chief Visionary Officer of Freedom LifeX Group. A CERTIFIED FINANCIAL PLANNER® with an MBA, he is also a trainer and the author of the book “Your IQ is not equal to your FQ”.

He is part of the founding group of financial professionals in Mumbai who came together with a shared goal of transforming lives through sound financial practices, and he co-founded the firm with his wife, Mrs. Shreedevi Gowda.

As Chief Visionary Officer, Swamy Sir steers the Finowise vision and culture — educating, inspiring and empowering people to get the right amount of money at the right point of time, so they can live a Happy & Freedom Life.
