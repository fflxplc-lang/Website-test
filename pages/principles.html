Guiding Principles

The 7 Ps That Guide Everything We Do

Purpose · People · Passion · Product · Process · Profit · Progress

1

Purpose

It helps us to live a purposeful and meaningful life on Earth. This is the reason why we wake up every day, why we exist and why we work.

Everyone should be clear about their own IKIGAI and the purpose of our life and business — “Why We Do What We Do”. It keeps us BONDED together towards SOLVING a larger problem and ensures we do not get derailed.

2

People

Business is not run by a person, it is run by People — and hence the well-being of THE TEAM is the building block of our organization.

We always follow the principle of People Priority. If we have to choose between Product, Profit and People, we always choose People.

3

Passion

Passion & Pagalpan create the INTENSE DRIVE within to achieve more than we can imagine.

To achieve anything significant we need to be energetic, and for that we need to be Passionate and demonstrate Pagalpan.

4

Product

Product is THE OUTCOME of our efforts towards SOLVING THE ULTIMATE challenge or problem.

We offer only those Products which serve and solve the P.A.I.N.s of People.

5

Process

People might fail, but Process and System work. It ensures consistency and continuity irrespective of the leader’s presence or absence.

This builds the organization and makes THE BRAND MAGNETIC — and hence we always respect the Process.

6

Profit

Profit is the fuel that ensures SUSTAINABILITY of the organization and THE TRIBE.

Hence, whatever we do, we need to ensure we are profitable.

7

Progress

The day you stop growing is the day you start dying — hence we ensure that we are constantly growing by discovering and becoming a better version of ourselves.

It ensures that even if the ecosystem changes, we REMAIN RELEVANT at all times. Hence we work only with those clients where we can make a meaningful difference in their life.
