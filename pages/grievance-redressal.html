SEBI (SCORES / SMART ODR)

If your complaint is still not resolved, you may lodge it with SEBI through SEBI SCORES (toll-free 1800 22 7575) or the SMART ODR portal.

What to Include

Your full name, registered mobile/email, folio or transaction reference (if any), and a clear description of the issue.
